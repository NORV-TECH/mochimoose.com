Effective September 29, 2026

Mochi Moose ("we", "us") makes family-friendly games for phones and tablets. This policy explains what information our games and this website use, and why. It applies to Solitaire Bible 3D and to every game we publish on Google Play, the Amazon Appstore and the Apple App Store. Questions or requests: support@mochimoose.com.

The short version
  • Our games have no accounts and never ask for your name, email, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • Your progress stays on your device. We don't run servers that collect it.
  • The free versions show ads from Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers to show and measure ads.
  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • You can delete everything at any time by resetting your progress or uninstalling the game.

Information that stays on your device

Your progress, settings, statistics, in-game currency (such as manna), the collectibles you find (such as crosses and sayings) and any note you write on a gift card are saved only on your device. They are not sent to us. If device backup is turned on (for example Google, Amazon or iCloud backup), your device may include this data in your own backup, under that company's policy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free versions of our games show ads from Google AdMob. For example, Solitaire Bible 3D shows a full-screen ad between some hands and an optional video you can choose to watch for a reward. To show and measure ads, limit how often you see them, prevent fraud and fix problems, Google's ad software may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device and app identifiers
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • device and app information (such as model, operating system and language) and how you interact with ads
  • diagnostic and crash information

Ads in our games are limited to family-safe content. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Ad privacy. On iPhone and iPad, personalized ads are shown only if you allow tracking when the app asks (Apple's App Tracking Transparency). On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads on some devices). On Fire tablets you can turn off interest-based ads in the device's privacy settings. Learn how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in Google's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Purchases such as "Remove ads" are processed by Google Play, Amazon or Apple. We never see your payment details. The store only tells the game that a purchase is complete so it can unlock it, including after you reinstall.

Sharing pictures and gifts

When you share a verse, a saying or a gift card, the game draws the picture on your device and hands it to the app you choose (for example WhatsApp or Messages). A note you write on a gift card is drawn into that picture. We don't receive the picture, the note, or who you send it to.

Children

Our games are made for a general audience and are not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Selling and sharing

We don't sell your personal information. Apart from Google's advertising services and the app stores described above, we don't share it with anyone. Under some U.S. state laws, such as California's, letting an ad network use device identifiers to personalize ads can count as "sharing". You can opt out of that at any time by turning off personalized ads on your device, as described above, or by writing to us.

Your choices and rights

Because our games keep your data on your device, you are in control: use Settings → Reset progress in the game, or uninstall the game, to delete it. For information collected by Google, use Google's controls linked above.

Depending on where you live (for example the European Union, the United Kingdom, Brazil or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or limit how it is used, and to withdraw your consent. Write to us at support@mochimoose.com and we will help. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

In the European Union and the United Kingdom, the legal bases for this processing are your consent (for personalized ads), our legitimate interest in showing non-personalized ads, measuring them and preventing fraud, and the performance of a contract (for purchases).

How long data is kept

Data on your device stays there until you reset the game or uninstall it. Google keeps advertising data as described in its own policies.

This website

mochimoose.com uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers, and its fonts are served from the site itself. If you choose a language, your browser remembers that choice on your own device. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may record visitors' IP addresses to keep the service secure and running (see the GitHub Privacy Statement). If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

International users

Google and the app stores may process information in the United States and other countries.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.

Los anuncios de nuestros juegos se limitan a contenido apto para toda la familia. En Android puedes restablecer o eliminar tu ID de publicidad en los ajustes del dispositivo (Ajustes → Privacidad → Anuncios, o Ajustes → Google → Anuncios en algunos dispositivos). En las tabletas Fire puedes desactivar los anuncios basados en intereses en los ajustes de privacidad del dispositivo. Más información sobre cómo Google usa estos datos en policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites y en la Política de privacidad de Google.

La nota que escribas en una tarjeta de regalo se dibuja dentro de esa imagen. No recibimos la imagen, ni la nota, ni a quién se la envías.

Menores

Nuestros juegos están hechos para el público general y no están dirigidos a menores de 13 años. No recopilamos a sabiendas información personal de menores. Si crees que un menor nos dio información personal, escríbenos y te ayudaremos a eliminarla.

Venta y uso compartido

No vendemos tu información personal. Aparte de los servicios de publicidad de Google y de las tiendas de apps descritos arriba, no la compartimos con nadie. Según algunas leyes estatales de EE. UU., como la de California, permitir que una red de anuncios use identificadores del dispositivo para personalizar anuncios puede considerarse "compartir". Puedes oponerte en cualquier momento desactivando los anuncios personalizados en tu dispositivo, como se explica arriba, o escribiéndonos.

Tus opciones y derechos

Como nuestros juegos guardan tus datos en tu dispositivo, tú tienes el control: usa Ajustes → Reiniciar progreso en el juego, o desinstálalo, para borrarlos. Para la información que recopila Google, usa los controles de Google indicados arriba.

Según dónde vivas (por ejemplo la Unión Europea, el Reino Unido, Brasil o California), puedes tener derecho a acceder, corregir o eliminar tu información personal, a oponerte a su uso o limitarlo, y a retirar tu consentimiento. Escríbenos a support@mochimoose.com y te ayudaremos. También puedes presentar una queja ante la autoridad de protección de datos de tu país.

En la Unión Europea y el Reino Unido, las bases legales de este tratamiento son tu consentimiento (para los anuncios personalizados), nuestro interés legítimo en mostrar anuncios no personalizados, medirlos y prevenir fraudes, y la ejecución de un contrato (para las compras).

Cuánto tiempo se guardan los datos

Los datos de tu dispositivo se quedan ahí hasta que reinicies el juego o lo desinstales. Google conserva los datos de publicidad según sus propias políticas.

Este sitio web

mochimoose.com no usa cookies, ni analítica, ni rastreadores, y sus fuentes se sirven desde el propio sitio. Si eliges un idioma, tu navegador recuerda esa elección en tu propio dispositivo.
